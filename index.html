Exploring Children's Drawings

Interactive tools for exploring a large dataset of children's drawings — tens of thousands of sketches across dozens of object categories, produced by children ages 2 to 10. See how drawings of the same concept change with age, watch them unfold stroke by stroke, and examine how a model recognizes them.

Data were collected through interactive drawing games at the San Jose Children's Discovery Museum and at Birch Aquarium in San Diego.

Explorers

Explore the full dataset

About 37,770 drawings from 48 categories laid out in CLIP embedding space and colored by model recognizability. Filter by category, age, and recognizability, and recompute the layout on any selection.

Stroke by stroke

Watch drawings render one stroke at a time, with each stroke colored by its semantic part (body, ear, wheel) and inter-annotator agreement. Compare two categories side by side.

Drawings with descriptions

Children's drawings of sea animals shown alongside the descriptions children gave of what they drew, linking how a concept is drawn to how it is described.
